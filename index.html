I work where design and engineering meet, and refuse to let either one carry the other.

For the last eight years I’ve moved between design tools and a code editor, translating layouts into interfaces that hold their shape on every screen. Most of my work starts as a page structure and ends as a system. [PLACEHOLDER]

I care about typography, spacing and the small decisions that make a site feel authored rather than assembled. I care just as much about load time, keyboard access and the fact that a beautiful page that janks is not beautiful. [PLACEHOLDER]
